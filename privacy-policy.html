Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Mind Your Block ("we," "us," "our") is a community-run initiative that helps volunteers organize and track neighborhood cleanup efforts in Deutschtown, Pittsburgh. This policy explains what information the Mind Your Block website and mobile app collect, how it's used, and the choices you have. We built this for our neighbors, not to build a data business — we collect the minimum needed to run the program.

Information We Collect

Account information (website and mobile app). To use Mind Your Block you create an account. We store your email address, a username, and your password (stored only as a one-way hash by our authentication provider; we cannot read it). Your username is your public display name and is what other users see on the leaderboard and activity feed. You can also give an optional first and last name, which is shown only on your own profile. If you add a profile photo or neighborhood, we store those too.

Check-in details. When you check in — on the website or in the mobile app — we record the zone or block you cleaned, how long you cleaned for, how many bags you collected, and any notes you add. For select geofenced sponsor events, we also record your GPS coordinates at the moment of verification. You can mark a check-in anonymous, which hides your name from other users (not from us).

Photos. If you attach a photo to a check-in, it's stored so progress can be seen. In the mobile app, check-in photos are uploaded to private storage that only your account can read; other users cannot see them. A profile photo, if you add one, is visible to other users.

Location. The mobile app asks for location access to automatically detect which zone or block you're standing in when you check in. In the app, your location is used on your phone only for that moment and is not sent to us or stored. The website uses location to verify attendance at certain sponsored events, and in that case the coordinates are recorded with your check-in. We do not track your movements in the background.

Camera, RSVPs & feedback. The mobile app asks for camera access so you can attach cleanup photos to a check-in, and we never access it at any other time. If you RSVP to an event or send us feedback, we store what you submit (and your email, if provided) to plan events and follow up.

How We Use Your Information

To operate check-ins, the leaderboard, mascot badges, block adoptions, and event RSVPs; to verify attendance at sponsored events; to respond to feedback; and to improve the app and website. We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

Where Your Information Is Stored

Your account, check-ins, RSVPs, block adoptions, and photos are stored in our database and file storage, hosted on Supabase, for both the website and the mobile app. The mobile app also keeps a local copy on your device so it works offline, and sends it when you are back online. Signing out of the app clears that local copy.

Sharing

We don't share your personal information with third parties, except service providers that host our infrastructure (like Supabase) under standard confidentiality terms, or if required by law. Map tiles load from OpenStreetMap, and any donation buttons link out to Venmo. Those services run under their own privacy policies, and the map provider can see your device's IP address when tiles load.

Children's Privacy

Mind Your Block is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your Choices

Check in anonymously to hide your name from the public feed and leaderboard. Delete your account and data: in the mobile app, open the You tab and choose Delete my data; this permanently removes your account, check-ins, RSVPs, adoptions, and photos. You can also email us at the address below from the email on your account and we will delete it. We may suspend accounts that post abusive names or false check-ins.
