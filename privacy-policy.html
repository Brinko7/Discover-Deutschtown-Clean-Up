Privacy Policy

Effective July 28, 2026

Mind Your Block ("we," "us," "our") is a community-run initiative that helps volunteers organize and track neighborhood cleanup efforts in Deutschtown, Pittsburgh. This policy explains what information the Mind Your Block website and mobile app collect, how it's used, and the choices you have. We built this for our neighbors, not to build a data business — we collect the minimum needed to run the program.

Information We Collect

Account information (website). If you create an account on the website, we store your name, email address, and profile photo (if you upload one) so you can sign in, earn points, and appear on the leaderboard.

Check-in details. When you check in — on the website or in the mobile app — we record the zone or block you cleaned, how long you cleaned for, how many bags you collected, and any notes you add. For select geofenced sponsor events, we also record your GPS coordinates at the moment of verification. You can mark a check-in anonymous, which hides your name from other users (not from us).

Photos. If you attach a photo to a check-in, it's stored so progress can be seen. In the mobile app, photos currently stay on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

Location. The mobile app asks for location access to automatically detect which zone or block you're standing in when you check in, and the website uses location to verify attendance at certain sponsored events. Location is only used in the moment it's requested — we do not track your movements in the background.

Camera, RSVPs & feedback. The mobile app asks for camera access so you can attach cleanup photos to a check-in, and we never access it at any other time. If you RSVP to an event or send us feedback, we store what you submit (and your email, if provided) to plan events and follow up.

How We Use Your Information

To operate check-ins, the leaderboard, mascot badges, block adoptions, and event RSVPs; to verify attendance at sponsored events; to respond to feedback; and to improve the app and website. We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

Where Your Information Is Stored

Website: account and check-in data is stored in our database (currently hosted on Supabase). Mobile app: as of this version, the app does not have its own account system — your points, check-ins, badges, and RSVPs are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us. If that changes, we'll update this policy first.

Sharing

We don't share your personal information with third parties, except service providers that host our infrastructure (like Supabase) under standard confidentiality terms, or if required by law. Donation buttons link out to Venmo, and map tiles load from OpenStreetMap/CARTO — those run under their own privacy policies once you leave our app or site.

Children's Privacy

Mind Your Block is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your Choices

Check in anonymously to hide your name from the public feed and leaderboard. Email us any time to request deletion of your account and associated data. On the mobile app, uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage) removes all locally stored data.
